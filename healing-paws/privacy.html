Healing Paws privacy policy

DRAFT — not yet published or effective. Requires factual, policy and legal review before public release. Prepared for the current two-pet beta, app version 1.0.0, build 1.

Who operates Healing Paws

Healing Paws is operated by Robert Sweeney under the InventJoy brand. InventJoy is a brand name, not an LLC or corporation. Domain: inventjoyapps.com. Support: support@inventjoyapps.com. This website and policy are prepared locally; publication is pending.

Purpose and audience

The initial beta is intended only for adults age 18 or older in the United States. Healing Paws offers wellness and emotional-support tools, not medical diagnosis or treatment, and cannot handle emergencies. U.S. crisis resources in the app are intended for people in the United States and are available without payment. Phone and messaging actions require confirmation; the app never calls or sends messages automatically.

Information saved locally

The app uses ordinary device preferences, not an encrypted journal or secure vault. It saves:

  • A boolean acknowledgement that you affirm the adult U.S. beta requirements. No birth date, identification, age value, location lookup or acknowledgement timestamp is collected.
  • Onboarding completion, selected pet, both companion names, support style, Pet sounds and Haptics preferences, and a legacy pet-selection value.
  • The most recent mood rating and date. There is no written journal or long-term mood-rating timeline.
  • The current or most recently saved daily routine’s mood, care and calming choices, celebrated routine dates, and pending reward-delivery records.
  • Per-pet Bond activity counts and event identifiers. These identifiers can contain dates or timestamps to prevent duplicate rewards. There is no automatic age/count purge of Bond records or celebrated routine dates.
  • The latest 100 completed comfort sessions: identifier, completion time, duration and better/same/worse outcome. The selected need, companion name and written thoughts are not stored in these session records.
  • The latest 100 completed bedtime sessions: identifier, completion time, selected duration and pet type.

Active clocks, unsaved edits and animations are held in memory; unfinished sessions are not saved as resumable sessions. Reduced motion follows the device setting. Artwork and the original purr are bundled resources, not recordings of you.

No account, app cloud sync or tracking

The current beta has no accounts, app-operated cloud syncing, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK or app server receiving wellness activity. The app does not request microphone, camera, photo, contact or location access. Debug builds include development-network tooling; device diagnostics and system services are separate from app features.

Retention, reset and backups

Preferences remain until changed, reset or uninstall, subject to the session limits above and operating-system backups. Settings → About, Privacy & Safety → Reset Healing Paws requires two confirmations and clears this installation’s saved onboarding, eligibility acknowledgement, preferences, companion information, mood, routines, histories and Bond/reward records. Canceling either step keeps the data. Other apps, operating-system settings, existing backups and bundled resources are not erased.

Deleting the app removes its current local installation data; offloading on iOS can retain documents and data. Android backup is not explicitly disabled or customized; iOS preferences are not explicitly excluded from backups. System backup or device transfer may retain or restore information, including eligibility acceptance. Reset cannot delete those copies. No backup-policy change has been applied, and deletion is not a promise of forensic secure erasure.

Actions you choose outside the app

Confirmed phone or messaging links pass only the destination number to another app, without a prefilled message. Email support passes only support@inventjoyapps.com and the fixed subject “Healing Paws Support,” without body, private data, device identifiers or attachments. Nothing is sent automatically. Copy email address writes only the public support address to the clipboard at your request. Your external apps and service providers apply their own practices.

Support email

support@inventjoyapps.com currently receives through tested forwarding; professional outbound sending is pending. Anything you voluntarily email is outside the app’s local preferences and is handled by email services. Support-inbox access, retention, response practices and formal privacy-inquiry handling remain for review before publication. No response or deletion timeline is promised. This inbox is not an emergency service.
