Email support

This opens your email app with only the recipient and the subject “Healing Paws Support.” It sends nothing automatically. If an email app does not open, select and copy the address above into your preferred email service.

Support and privacy questions

Email support@inventjoyapps.com for help with the app, privacy questions, or requests about support correspondence. The address currently receives messages through forwarding; replies from this address are not yet available. No response time is guaranteed.

Share only what you choose

A brief description of an app problem is enough to start. Please avoid sending sensitive mood or personal information, private identifiers or attachments. No private app or device data is prefilled. Email is handled by your email provider and the support mail service, separately from the app’s local data.

Need urgent human help?

Healing Paws and the support inbox cannot handle emergencies. In the United States, call 911 if you are in immediate danger or need emergency assistance. For emotional distress or a suicidal crisis in the United States, call or text 988. Outside the United States, contact local emergency services or a local crisis service.

The app’s U.S. crisis resources are available without payment. Phone and messaging actions in the app require confirmation before another app opens.
