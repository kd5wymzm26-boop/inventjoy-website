Email support

This opens your email app with only the recipient and the subject “Healing Paws Support.” It sends nothing automatically. If an email app does not open, select and copy the address above into your preferred email service.

Current support readiness

The address currently receives email through tested forwarding. Professional outbound sending from this address is still being prepared. No response time is promised. Inbox access, retention and privacy-inquiry handling need review before release.

Share only what you choose

A brief description of an app problem is enough to start. Please avoid sending sensitive mood or personal information, private identifiers or attachments. No private app or device data is prefilled. Email is handled by your email provider and the support mail service, separately from the app’s local data.

Need urgent human help?

Healing Paws and this inbox cannot handle emergencies. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. The app includes U.S. human-support information with confirmation before any phone or messaging link opens.
