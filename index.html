InventJoy · Healing Paws

A little room for calm.
A companion by your side.

Meet Healing Paws: a warm, two-companion wellness app with small moments of comfort throughout your day.

Choose a Golden Retriever or gray tabby, give your companion a name, and spend time together at your own pace.

Read the draft privacy policySupport information

Beta preparation: intended for adults 18 and older in the United States. No download or public availability is announced here.

Everyday companionship

Pet, walk, breathe or rest with your companion. A daily mood check-in and simple care routine offer gentle ways to spend time together.

Quiet moments, your way

Comfort Me Now and Bedtime With Your Pet offer guided or quiet time. Sound starts off, and reduced motion follows the device setting.

Progress without pressure

Our Bond celebrates time shared. Bond never decreases because you stay away. Companion choices, preferences and progress are saved locally, subject to operating-system backups.

Wellness, with clear limits

Healing Paws provides wellness and emotional-support tools, not medical diagnosis or treatment. It cannot handle emergencies or replace human help. Support email is not an emergency service. The app’s U.S. crisis resources are intended for people in the United States.
